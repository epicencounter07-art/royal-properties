Apartments for Sale in Westlands

If you are looking for apartments for sale in Westlands, Nairobi, Royal Vista Residence offers a selection of modern 1, 2 and 3 bedroom apartments along Waiyaki Way.

Royal Vista Residence is a 27-storey residential development offering different apartment sizes for buyers looking for a modern home or investment property in Westlands. Prices currently start from KES 11.4 million, with completion scheduled for the end of 2027.

1 Bedroom Apartments in Westlands

Royal Vista Residence offers 1 bedroom apartments in Westlands in two size options. The 78 sqm apartments are priced from KES 11.4M, while the 88 sqm apartments are priced from KES 12.8M, subject to the applicable unit and floor.

These apartments provide an option for buyers looking for a modern one-bedroom home or an investment property in Westlands, Nairobi.

2 Bedroom Apartments in Westlands

The development also offers 2 bedroom apartments measuring approximately 157 sqm. The apartments are all ensuite and are listed from KES 22.5M, with pricing varying by the applicable range.

The larger floor area provides additional living space for buyers looking for a spacious apartment in Westlands.

3 Bedroom Apartments in Westlands

Royal Vista Residence also offers 3 bedroom apartments in Westlands. The 133 sqm option is listed at KES 20.5M, while the 3 bedroom apartment with DSQ measures approximately 186 sqm and is listed at KES 28.5M.
